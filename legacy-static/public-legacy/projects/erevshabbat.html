Ending late-stage oral cancer through AI-powered chair-side early detection

Open in App

EREVSHABBAT is an AI-assisted screening project focused on earlier oral cancer detection during routine dental visits.

The goal is to reduce late-stage diagnoses by helping clinicians identify suspicious lesions earlier and escalate care faster.

  • Chair-side workflow: Fast image capture that fits into routine appointments.
  • Risk scoring: AI-assisted prioritization of lesions for follow-up.
  • Clinical support: Clear referral guidance for higher-risk findings.
  • Screening consistency: Standardized checks across visits and clinics.

Move oral cancer detection earlier in the care journey so patients can receive treatment sooner and outcomes can improve.
